# Cold-coast PBR materials

All six downloaded maps are Creative Commons Zero (CC0), supplied by Poly Haven.

- **Coast Sand 02**, Rob Tuytel: https://polyhaven.com/a/coast_sand_02
- **Rock Boulder Dry**, photography by Dimitrios Savva, processing by Rico Cilliers:
  https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_dry

Diffuse and OpenGL normal maps: 2K JPG. Roughness maps: 1K JPG.
`sources.json` records the exact download URLs, sizes and SHA-256 hashes.
The game applies wet, subdued material colors in its shader; source files are
preserved unchanged. License: https://polyhaven.com/license
