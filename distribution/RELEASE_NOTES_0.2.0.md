# FLECKY Demo v0.2.0

The first public Windows demo of **FLECKY: Adventures in Icy Waters**.

## How to play

1. Download `FLECKY_Demo_0.2.0_Windows.zip`.
2. Extract the entire folder.
3. Run `FLECKY.exe`.

Godot and an internet connection are not required.

## Included

- a complete 10-fish adventure in the icy bay;
- surface swimming, diving, boosting and water breaches;
- an animated shark threat with safe ice floes;
- three fish species, ambient schools, crabs and an octopus cave;
- free-roam mode after completing the main objective;
- eight persistent achievements, including secret discoveries;
- English, Ukrainian and Russian localization;
- fullscreen/windowed modes and selectable resolution.

Игра готова к запуску на Windows 10/11 x64: распакуйте архив и откройте
`FLECKY.exe`.
